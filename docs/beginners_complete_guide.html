🤖 Your 5-Person AI Trading Team

Instead of one AI making all the decisions, this system divides the work among 5 specialized agents. Think of them as 5 different people, each with one job. No agent can override another's safety rules.

🔍 Agent 1 — The Scout
Market Regime & Technical Analyst
What it looks at: The last 100 hours of Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and XRP price history. It calculates trend lines, price momentum, and how volatile (choppy) the market is.

What it outputs: A simple verdict like "Bitcoin is STRONG BULLISH" or "XRP is STRONG BEARISH — market is falling."

Critical Rule: The Scout is read-only. It never places orders. It only reports what it sees.
↓
💡 Agent 2 — The Planner
Strategy & Setup Generator
What it does: Reads the Scout's report and searches for a smart entry point.

The 2-for-1 Rule: Every trade idea must have a clear path to earn at least $2 in profit for every $1 risked. If no such setup exists, the Planner stays quiet — no trade is generated.

What it outputs: A specific proposal: "Buy Bitcoin at $80,950. Safety exit at $79,350. Profit target at $81,760."
↓
🛡️ Agent 3 — The Risk Boss (Most Important!)
Risk & Discipline Gatekeeper
Why it matters most: The Risk Boss has absolute veto power. No trade can proceed without its approval.

Its 3 strict checks:
  ①  "Did we already lose 5% of the account today?" → If yes: ALL TRADING STOPS for the day.
  ②  "Do we already have 3 active trades?" → If yes: REJECTED. Too crowded.
  ③  "Would this trade risk more than 1% of our balance?" → If yes: REJECTED. Too big.

What it outputs: A green [APPROVED] stamp (with exact contract size) or a red [REJECTED] stamp.
↓ Only if APPROVED
🚀 Agent 4 — The Pilot
Execution & Order Manager
What it does: Takes the approved trade plan and communicates directly with Delta Exchange to place the order.

How it places orders: It sends a 3-in-1 Bracket Order. In a single action, it places:
  • Your entry order (buying Bitcoin at the right price).
  • An automatic Stop-Loss order (emergency exit to prevent big loss).
  • An automatic Take-Profit order (target exit to lock in winnings).

Safety Rule: The Pilot refuses to place any order without an attached Stop-Loss. Never a naked trade.
↓
📊 Agent 5 — The Accountant
Portfolio Monitor & Memory Journaler
What it does: Once a trade is running, the Accountant watches it live. When the price moves far enough into profit, it triggers the Breakeven Protocol: it moves the Stop-Loss up to your exact purchase price.

Result: You now have a "Free-Roll" — the worst case is you break even ($0 loss). Best case, the price keeps moving and you collect the full profit.

It also writes a diary: Every trade detail is saved to state/trade_journal.json and a daily summary is generated in reports/.

🔑 The 5 Golden Rules for Beginners

  • 🧪
    Always Start on Testnet (Demo) First Keep DELTA_ENVIRONMENT=testnet_india in your .env file for at least 1–2 weeks. Watch the bot trade, check the results, build confidence. Only then switch to live.
  • 🔐
    Never Enable "Withdrawal" on Your API Keys When creating API keys on Delta Exchange, only check Read and Trade. Never Withdraw. Even if your keys were ever leaked, funds could never leave your exchange account.
  • 💰
    Never Touch the 1% Risk Rule Keep MAX_RISK_PER_TRADE_PCT=0.01. Even after 10 bad trades in a row, you would still have 90% of your money. Only increase this once you have at least 3 months of profitable history.
  • 🛑
    Never Manually Cancel a Stop-Loss Without Replacing It The stop-loss is your safety airbag. If you remove it without putting a new one, one bad candle could wipe your entire position. If you want to adjust it, always place the new stop first.
  • 🚨
    Know Your Panic Button If Bitcoin crashes suddenly or major news breaks, say in chat: "Close all positions and cancel all orders immediately." The bot will instantly market-sell everything and cancel all pending orders.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can I lose more than 1% of my balance on a single trade?
No. The Risk Boss calculates contract sizes so that even if the stop-loss is hit, the maximum loss is capped at 1% of your available account balance. This is mathematically enforced before the order is placed.
Q. What happens if the exchange goes offline while I have an open trade?
Your stop-loss and take-profit orders are stored on Delta Exchange's matching engine, not on your computer. If your internet goes down or the bot stops, your protection orders are already in place and will still execute.
Q. Can the bot lose all my money?
The circuit breaker will halt all trading if your account drops 5% in a day. Between that and the 1% per-trade risk rule, even 10 consecutive bad trades would only reduce your account by roughly 10%, not wipe it out.
Q. Do I need to watch the screen while the bot trades?
No. Once a trade is placed, all the exit orders (stop-loss and take-profit) live on Delta Exchange's servers. You can close the app, your laptop, or even sleep. The exits will fire automatically.
Q. Which coins does the bot trade?
By default: Bitcoin (BTCUSDT), Ethereum (ETHUSDT), Solana (SOLUSDT), and Ripple (XRPUSDT). You can change the list in your .env file: DEFAULT_SYMBOLS=BTCUSDT,ETHUSDT,SOLUSDT,XRPUSDT
Q. What does "Free-Roll" mean?
After a trade moves into profit, the bot moves the stop-loss up to your exact purchase price. Now you have zero downside risk — if the market reverses, you exit at your entry and lose $0. If it continues, you hit the profit target.
