1 System Overview & Core Philosophy

The Delta Exchange Multi-Agent Trading System is built on the core principle of strict capital preservation inspired by claude-trading-skills. It solves the critical problem of emotional, unhedged, and undisciplined trading by separating trade decisions into five specialized, non-overlapping autonomous agents.

⚠️ Non-Negotiable Institutional Rule
No trade is ever placed blindly or naked. Every single executed trade must satisfy mathematical regime filters, pass the Risk Gatekeeper's daily drawdown circuit breaker, enforce exact 1% account risk sizing, and transmit an atomic bracket Stop-Loss and Take-Profit protection order simultaneously.

1. Market Regime Analyst Multi-timeframe trend & volatility classification. EMA 9/21/50/200, RSI-14, ATR-14, MACD, Pivot S/R Strictly read-only; never suggests orders.

3 Technical Analysis & Quantitative Edge

The Regime Analyst evaluates the last 100 hourly candlesticks (resolution: 60) across watched perpetual futures pairs (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, XRPUSDT):

1. Exponential Moving Average (EMA) Ribbon Stack Trend Filter

Calculates EMA 9, 21, 50, and 200. When $\text{EMA}_9 > \text{EMA}_{21} > \text{EMA}_{50} > \text{EMA}_{200}$ and price is above the 9 EMA, the regime is classified as STRONG_BULLISH. Counter-trend trades are strictly forbidden.

2. Momentum & Volatility (RSI-14 & ATR-14) Exhaustion Filter

- RSI-14 (Relative Strength Index): Filters out overbought FOMO chases ($>72$) and oversold panic dumps ($<28$). Healthy continuation trades require RSI between $45 \text{ and } 65$.
- ATR-14 (Average True Range): Measures true candle volatility to place stops that withstand normal market chop:

True Range (TR) = max(High - Low, |High - Close_prev|, |Low - Close_prev|)
ATR_14 = Simple Moving Average of TR over 14 periods
3. Dynamic Stop Loss & Target Math Alpha Edge

The invalidation level (Stop Loss) is dynamically anchored to structural trend support plus volatility buffer:

Stop Loss (Long) = max(EMA_21 * 0.995, Entry - 1.5 * ATR_14)
Risk Distance = Entry - Stop Loss
Take Profit Target = Entry + (Risk Distance * 2.0)   [Guarantees 2:1 R:R]

4 Risk Management Engine & Circuit Breaker

The Risk Gatekeeper acts as a strict, emotionless trading desk manager. If a candidate setup fails any single risk criterion, the trade is discarded instantly.

Discipline Rule Threshold / Formula Purpose & Impact
Daily Drawdown Circuit Breaker $\text{Drawdown} \ge 5.0\%$ of starting equity Haults all trading for the day to prevent revenge trading or catastrophic black swans.
Position Risk Ceiling $\text{Max Risk} = \text{Account Equity} \times 0.01$ (1%) Never risks more than 1% of total equity on any single trade invalidation.
Mathematical Sizing $\text{Contracts} = \frac{\text{Risk Amount}}{\text{Distance to Stop} \times \text{Contract Value}}$ Dynamically scales contract size up when stop is tight and down when stop is wide.
Leverage Ceiling Max $10\times$ leverage (Default $5\times$) Prevents liquidation wipes; liquidation distance is maintained $> 15\%$ away.
Concurrent Positions Max 3 open positions simultaneously Caps total portfolio correlation and margin exposure.
